Welcome!

This tutorial will help you practice identifying the four types of operant conditioning.

Remember these two questions:
  1. Does the consequence make the behavior increase or decrease?
  2. Is something added or taken away?

Reinforcement increases a behavior. Punishment decreases a behavior.

Positive means something is added. Negative means something is removed.

What to remember

  • Positive reinforcement: Add something desirable; behavior increases.
  • Negative reinforcement: Remove something unpleasant; behavior increases.
  • Positive punishment: Add something unpleasant; behavior decreases.
  • Negative punishment: Remove something desirable; behavior decreases.
